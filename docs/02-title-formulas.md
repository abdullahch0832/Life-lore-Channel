# 02 — Title Engine: The Repeatable Formulas

Every RealLifeLore modern title is a **curiosity gap**: it states something that feels
wrong/impossible/alarming and withholds the explanation. Your job is to pick a topic,
then press it into one of these proven molds.

## The 7 core formulas (with live examples)

### F1 — The "Empty" paradox *(flagship series — most repeatable)*
> **"Why [N]% of [Place] is Empty"**

- Why 95% of Australia is Empty (14M)
- Why 70% of Spain is Empty (12M)
- Why 80% of New Zealand is Empty (10M)
- Why 82% of Mexico is Empty (8.9M)
- Why 99.7% of Mongolia is Completely Empty (8.6M)
- Why Kazakhstan is Insanely Empty (8.6M)

**Why it works:** a precise shocking number + a paradox (a whole country, mostly empty?).
The % makes it feel like data, not opinion. **You can run this ~50 times** (one per country/region).

### F2 — Hidden / concealed
> **"What's Hidden Under the Ice/Trees/Sand of [Place]?"**

- What's Hidden Under the Ice of Antarctica? (11M)
- What's Hidden Under the Ice of Greenland? (7.2M)
- What's Hidden Under the Trees of the Amazon Rainforest? (7.2M)

**Why it works:** mystery box. Promises a reveal. Also a clean series.

### F3 — Danger / forbidden
> **"Why Visiting [Place] Will Kill You"** / **"The [Place] You Can't Visit"**

- Why Visiting This Lost Island Will Kill You (9.7M)
- The Stone Age Tribe on a Banned Island You Can't Visit (11M)
- Why Nobody Can Stop North Korea (8.3M)

**Why it works:** fear + forbidden‑knowledge pull.

### F4 — Catastrophe / fatal flaw
> **"[Country]'s Catastrophic [X] Problem"**

- Russia's Catastrophic Oil & Gas Problem (7.7M)
- China's Catastrophic Oil & Gas Problem (7.5M)
- Saudi Arabia's Catastrophic "Everything" Problem (9M)

**Why it works:** conflict/schadenfreude + implied collapse stakes.

### F5 — Rise / superlative
> **"Why [Place] is Becoming [the most / the world's X]"**

- Why Texas is Becoming America's Most Powerful State (10M)
- Why Norway is Becoming the World's Richest Country (8M)

**Why it works:** superlatives + a trend you "need to understand now."

### F6 — Geopolitical cause/effect ("Why X [verb] Y")
> **"Why [actor] [surprising geopolitical claim]"** / **"How [geography] made [outcome]"**

- Why Finland Joining NATO Checkmates Russia (19M)
- Why Russia's Biggest Threat is Actually China (12M)
- Why 80% of Americans Live East of This Line (10M)
- How Geography Made The US Ridiculously OP (7.1M)

### F7 — Live current event
> **"Why [Country] is [doing X right now]"** / **"What Happens if [event]?"**

- Why Russia is Invading Ukraine (21M)
- What Happens if Iran Blocks the Strait of Hormuz? (8.4M)

**Why it works:** rides search + news demand. Use ~1×/month, fast turnaround.

### (Bonus) Era‑1 curiosity scale formula — great for shorter/cheaper videos
> **"[Thing] is Way [Bigger/Deeper] Than You Think"**, **"What if…"**, **"What's the most [extreme]…"**
- The Ocean is Way Deeper Than You Think (50M) · How the Universe is Way Bigger Than You Think (33M)

## Title rules (the mechanics)

1. **One idea only.** Never two hooks in one title.
2. **A concrete number or superlative** whenever possible (`95%`, `99.7%`, `Most Powerful`, `Richest`).
3. **Withhold the answer.** The title is the question; the video is the payoff.
4. **Front‑load the strange word**: *Empty, Hidden, Catastrophic, Kill, Impossible, Nobody.*
5. **Keep it ~5–9 words**, readable on mobile.
6. **Title + thumbnail must promise the same thing** (see `docs/04`). The thumbnail
   shows the *place*; the title delivers the *paradox*.
7. **Match an emotion**: curiosity, fear, paradox, conflict, or awe. If a title triggers
   none, kill it.

## Your title workflow

1. Have a topic (from `docs/06` idea mining).
2. Run it through **all 7 formulas** — write every variant.
3. Score each 1–5 on: *curiosity gap, specificity, emotion, mobile readability.*
4. Keep the top 2. These become your A/B thumbnail‑text options.
