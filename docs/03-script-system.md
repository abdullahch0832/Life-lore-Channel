# 03 — Script System: The Mystery → Why Arc

Reverse‑engineered from the full transcript of **"Why 70% of Spain is Empty"**
(2,503 words, 15:32, ≈160 words/minute).

## The 8‑beat skeleton

Every modern RealLifeLore essay follows the same emotional arc. Use this as a fill‑in
structure for every script.

| # | Beat | Job | Spain example | ~% of runtime |
|---|------|-----|---------------|---------------|
| 1 | **Impressive setup** | Make the subject sound big/normal/successful | "Spain, 47M people, 14th largest economy, 2nd most high‑speed rail on Earth…" | 0–3% |
| 2 | **The turn** | Hard pivot to the anomaly | "But here's the strange part…" | ~3% |
| 3 | **Name the mystery** | Give the paradox a vivid, ownable name | "a weird vacant **donut‑shaped population void**" | 3–8% |
| 4 | **The shocking headline stat** | One number that frames the whole video | "90% of Spaniards live on just 30% of the land" | 8–12% |
| 5 | **Zoom to a concrete example** | Pick one extreme sub‑case, make it relatable | "Serranía Celtibérica — the **'Spanish Lapland'**, size of Ireland, 500k people" | 12–30% |
| 6 | **The WHY — geography first** | Physical cause | Iberian mountain system, high elevation | 30–45% |
| 7 | **The WHY — history second** | Human cause, told as a story | Franco → civil war → 1959 industrialization → emigration → low fertility | 45–85% |
| 8 | **Stakes + resolution + sponsor** | Consequence, "what must happen", seamless ad | "a vicious cycle… the only fix is investment → *investing intelligently is the key* → **public.com**" | 85–100% |

## The writing DNA (copy these habits exactly)

1. **Every sentence carries a number or a comparison.** The script is a stream of
   concrete facts, never vague. Density = authority.
2. **Relatable yardsticks constantly.** Unknown things are measured against known ones:
   *"the same size as California"*, *"like the US state of Kansas"*, *"the population
   of Ireland."* Always convert foreign data into your audience's reference frame.
3. **Pattern‑interrupt phrases** drive the arc: *"But here's the strange part…"*,
   *"and that's not the only reason…"*, *"but mountains alone don't explain it."*
   These are retention hooks — one every 60–90 seconds.
4. **Name your concepts.** Coining a sticky label ("donut void", "Spanish Lapland")
   makes the idea memorable and shareable.
5. **One micro‑story / meme moment.** Spain's video includes the Teruel village whose
   train is so slow *a tractor overtook it* and it became a meme. Find one vivid, almost
   funny concrete anecdote per video — it's the clip people share.
6. **Cause stacking.** The "why" is never one reason. It's geography **+** history **+**
   policy **+** demographics, layered. This is what justifies the length.
7. **Seamless sponsor bridge.** The ad is introduced by a thematic sentence from the
   content itself, so it doesn't feel like a hard stop. (Spain: content ends on
   "investment is the fix" → sponsor is an *investing* app.)

## Length & pacing targets

| Your stage | Target length | Word count (@150–160 wpm) |
|---|---|---|
| New channel (recommended start) | 8–12 min | 1,200–1,900 |
| Proven retention | 15–25 min | 2,400–4,000 |
| RealLifeLore full scale | 30–50 min | 5,000–8,000 |

- **Hook must land the paradox in the first 20–30 seconds** (beats 1–2). Do not bury it.
- Add a pattern‑interrupt or new sub‑topic roughly every **60–90 seconds** to reset attention.

## Accuracy & sourcing discipline (don't skip — this protects the channel)

AI will draft the script fast, but **you must verify every statistic**. This niche lives
on trust; one viral wrong number invites "corrections" videos.

- Pull real figures from: **Wikipedia, World Bank, UN, CIA World Factbook, Our World in
  Data, national statistics offices, Encyclopedia Britannica, academic papers.**
- Keep a **source line next to every stat** in the script draft (see the template).
- Flag anything AI can't source → cut it or replace it.
- Round honestly ("~90%"), and avoid claims you can't back.

## How to produce a script

1. Fill [`../templates/script-template.md`](../templates/script-template.md) with your
   topic and chosen title.
2. Run [`../prompts/03-script-writer.md`](../prompts/03-script-writer.md) to draft all 8
   beats in the RealLifeLore voice.
3. **Fact‑check pass** — verify/annotate every number with a source.
4. **Read‑aloud pass** — cut anything that slows the first 30 seconds or sags in the
   middle; make sure a pattern‑interrupt lands every ~90s.
5. Mark **[B‑ROLL]** and **[MAP]** cues inline for the editor (the template has a column).
