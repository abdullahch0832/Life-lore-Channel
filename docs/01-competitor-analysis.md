## The two eras (this matters for you)

### Era 1 — "Scale & curiosity" shorts‑ish (2016–2018)
Short 5–9 minute videos built on one mind‑blowing scale/"what if" idea. These are
their all‑time view leaders because they had 8–9 years to accumulate:

| Video | Views | Len |
|---|---|---|
| The Ocean is Way Deeper Than You Think | 50M | 6:36 |
| What's the Deepest Hole We Can Possibly Dig? | 48M | 6:41 |
| How the Universe is Way Bigger Than You Think | 33M | 9:06 |
| These Are the Events That Will Happen Before 2050 | 25M | 9:24 |
| How the World Map Looks Wildly Different Than You Think | 25M | 6:20 |
| The Terrifying True Scale of Nuclear Weapons | 16M | 5:57 |

Pattern: **"[Thing] is way [bigger/deeper] than you think"**, **"What if…"**,
**"What's the most [extreme] …"**. Low production cost, pure curiosity gap.

### Era 2 — Long‑form geopolitics documentaries (2021 → now)
20–58 minute map‑essays on countries, conflicts and geographic anomalies. This is the
format you said you want to make, and it's where all recent growth + ad revenue is
(longer = more mid‑rolls, higher RPM).

> **Strategic note for a NEW channel:** start closer to **Era 1 length (8–15 min)** on
> **Era 2 topics**. You get the proven modern packaging but a realistic production load
> and better early retention. Scale length up as retention proves out.

### What the outliers prove
1. **Repeatable series beat one‑offs.** Three distinct patterns each produced multiple
   8M+ videos: the **"Empty country"** series, the **North Korea escape** series, and
   the **"What's Hidden Under the Ice/Trees of X"** series. Series = compounding.
2. **Topic emotion matters more than the country.** North Korea (fear/mystery), Russia‑
   Ukraine (current‑event stakes), "empty" (paradox/curiosity). The *feeling* is the product.
3. **Current events spike hardest.** "Why Russia is Invading Ukraine" rode a live news
   wave — evergreen templates + 1 timely topic per month is the balance.

## Their SEO reality (important & freeing)

- **Tags are generic and reused on every video** — just brand keywords like
  `real life lore`, `geography`, `world map is wrong`. They do **not** do per‑video
  keyword tags.
- **No uploaded captions** on the sampled video.
- Conclusion: **their growth engine is the PACKAGE (title + thumbnail) + browse/suggested,
  not tag SEO.** So your effort belongs in title/thumbnail/retention, not tag stuffing.
