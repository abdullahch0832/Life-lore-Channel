# 09 — PHASE 1: Start with the OLD short "curiosity" videos (6–15 min)

> **Your decided strategy:**
> **Phase 1 (now):** remake the *old* 6–15 min "scale / curiosity / what‑if" videos
> (the 2016–2018 ones that hit 33M–50M). Low production, evergreen, easy scripts.
> **Phase 2 (later, once the channel is built):** move to *recent / current‑event*
> geopolitics (what's happening in the last ~100 days).
> This file is all about Phase 1.

## Why this is the smart start
- **Evergreen:** "How deep is the ocean" is as interesting in 2026 as in 2017. These
  videos earn views for 9+ years — RealLifeLore's biggest are still these.
- **Cheap & fast:** 6–9 min, mostly simple graphics + stock, single idea. One person can
  make one in days, not weeks.
- **Easy script:** one simple "ladder / journey" structure (see `docs/10`), not a
  30‑minute research essay.
- **Broad appeal:** science/scale/"what if" beats niche geopolitics for a cold audience,
  so the algorithm can push it to everyone early.

## The 3 sub‑formats inside Phase 1
1. **Scale ladder** — "[Thing] is way bigger/deeper than you think." Climb a ladder of
   sizes/depths, each more extreme. (Ocean, Universe, Nuclear, Tsunamis.) **Easiest to
   write — start here.**
2. **"What if" scenario** — "What if [X happened]?" Walk through the consequences step by
   step. (100 people, ice melts, road around the world, Soviet reunion.)
3. **Journey / "the most extreme"** — "What's the most [remote/deep/difficult] [X]?"
   Travel from normal to the absolute extreme. (Most difficult place to reach, last place
   discovered, farthest from everyone.)

## Your Phase‑1 title formulas (reuse endlessly)
- **"[Thing] is Way [Bigger/Deeper/Hotter/Faster] Than You Think"**
- **"What's the [Deepest/Tallest/Most Extreme] [X] [Possible]?"**
- **"What If [X] Happened?"** / **"What If [impossible scenario]?"**
- **"How [Big/Far/Deep] is [X]?"** / **"What's the Most [extreme] [X] in the World?"**

Topic wells (never run out): space & planets, the ocean & deep sea, Earth's extremes
(volcanoes, deserts, caves, mountains), physics "what ifs", weapons/energy scale, time &
the far future, human population thought‑experiments, weird geography records.

## DON'T copy, DO remake
These exact topics are already done by RLL (and many copycats). Your edge:
- **A fresh angle or newer data** (e.g. "The Ocean is Deeper Than You Think" → **"How
  Deep Can Humans Actually Survive in the Ocean?"**).
- **Better, cleaner visuals** (`docs/05`).
- **A stronger package** (`docs/02`, `docs/04`).
Re‑research every fact yourself — never lift their script or footage.
