# 04 — Thumbnail System

The thumbnail + title is the whole product at the click stage. In this niche the
thumbnail's job is to show **the place** and **a single dramatic visual promise** while
the title delivers the paradox.

## Observed patterns (RealLifeLore + niche peers)

- **Resolution:** 1280×720 (maxres), designed to read at tiny mobile size.
- **One dominant subject:** a recognizable **country/continent silhouette or map**,
  often glowing or outlined, or a single dramatic scene (frozen continent, a lone
  island, a missile, a flag).
- **Bold short overlay text:** 1–4 words echoing the hook — e.g. `EMPTY`, `95%`,
  `HIDDEN`, `NORTH KOREA`. Heavy sans‑serif, ALL CAPS, thick stroke/shadow for contrast.
- **High‑contrast, saturated color.** Dark or desaturated background so the subject and
  text pop. Frequent use of **red** for danger/arrows/markers.
- **Directional cues:** arrows, circles, a glowing line, or a "line across the map"
  that visualizes the claim (e.g. the "80% live east of this line" divider).
- **Emotion in one glance:** paradox, danger, or mystery — never a neutral map.
- **Consistency:** a repeatable layout + font so your channel is recognizable in a feed
  (this is how a *series* compounds).

## The thumbnail rulebook

1. **Readable at 10% size.** Shrink it to a thumbnail‑of‑the‑thumbnail; if the subject +
   text aren't instantly clear, redo it.
2. **Max ~4 words of text**, and the words must NOT just repeat the title — they add the
   second punch (title says the sentence, thumb says the shock word + number).
3. **One focal point.** Country shape OR scene OR object — not all three.
4. **Contrast test:** subject vs background, text vs everything. If it's muddy, darken
   the background or add an outline/glow.
5. **Red = stakes.** Use sparingly for the danger/arrow/marker; don't flood it.
6. **Brand lock:** fix a font, a text‑placement zone, and a treatment (e.g. glowing
   outline map) and reuse it so a series looks like a series.
7. **Face optional.** RealLifeLore is faceless; many peers add a small shocked/leader
   face or a dramatic photo for emotion. Test it, but keep the map dominant.

## Layout templates (pick per formula)

| Formula | Thumbnail recipe |
|---|---|
| F1 "Empty" | Country silhouette, most of it shaded dark/void, small cluster of lights/dots where people live, big `N% EMPTY` |
| F2 "Hidden" | The place half‑revealed (ice cutaway, trees peeled back), glowing `?` or `HIDDEN` |
| F3 "Danger" | The place with red warning tint + `CAN'T VISIT` / skull / barrier |
| F4 "Catastrophe" | Country flag/map cracking or burning, downward red arrow, `COLLAPSE` / the X word |
| F5 "Rise" | Country glowing gold/green, up arrow, `#1` / `RICHEST` |
| F6/F7 Geopolitics | Two flags/countries + arrows/clash, or a map with a bold dividing line |

## Workflow

1. Draft **2 text options** from your top‑2 titles (`docs/02`).
2. Build the base (map/scene) — see tools in `docs/05`.
3. Apply the layout template for your formula.
4. Run the shrink test + contrast test.
5. Keep a `/thumbnails/` archive so your series stays visually consistent.

> **NexLev helpers:** `get_similar_thumbnails` (see what already ranks for your topic),
> and `generate_thumbnail` / `edit_thumbnail` (AI thumbnail drafts). See `docs/08`.
