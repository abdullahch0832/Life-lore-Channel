## The 4 idea wells

### Well 1 — Evergreen geographic/geopolitical anomalies (the bulk)
Any country or region has a "weird fact" that fits a formula from `docs/02`:
- Population paradoxes → F1 "Empty"
- Hidden geology/geography → F2 "Hidden"
- Resource/economic fragility → F4 "Catastrophic"
- Strategic importance → F6 geopolitics

Source list: Wikipedia "Geography of [country]", "Demographics of [country]", Our World
in Data, r/geography, r/MapPorn, atlas/almanac trivia. You will never run out.

### Well 2 — Live current events (~1 per month)
Wars, elections, blockades, sanctions, disputed borders. These spike hardest (see
"Why Russia is Invading Ukraine", 21M). Watch: AP/Reuters world desk, Google Trends
"geopolitics", a conflict‑tracker. Turn around fast while the topic is hot.

### Well 3 — Series re‑runs (compounding)
Take a title that already worked and swap the subject. "Empty" has ~50 countries left.
"What's Hidden Under the Ice of…" has every glaciated/forested region. One proven
template = a dozen videos.

### Well 4 — Competitor outlier mining ⭐ (your #1 well — do this weekly)
Find videos that massively out‑performed their channel's average **in this exact niche**,
then make your better version. This is where NexLev earns its keep.

## Turning a mined outlier into YOUR video (the clone‑and‑beat loop)
1. Find an outlier (NexLev — `docs/08` routine).
2. Identify *why it worked*: which formula, which emotion, what's the paradox?
3. **Don't copy the content — copy the demand.** Research the topic from scratch
   (`docs/03` sourcing) so yours is accurate and original.
4. Re‑package with your own stronger title (`docs/02`) + thumbnail (`docs/04`).
5. Out‑execute on edit quality (`docs/05`) and script tightness (`docs/03`).
6. Log it in your backlog with the source outlier noted.

## Ethics / safety line
Mine *ideas and demand signals*, never scripts, voiceover, or footage. Re‑research every
fact. Copying another creator's actual script/art is both a strike risk and a dead end —
the whole edge here is doing the topic *better*, not duplicating it.
